killtony-clipbot

A personal video-clipping tool

killtony-clipbot is a personal, single-user tool that helps prepare short vertical video clips from publicly available videos and upload them to the developer's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Selects short segments of a video and formats them for vertical (9:16) viewing.
  • Adds captions to the clips.
  • Uploads the finished clips to the developer's own YouTube channel as private videos, so they can be reviewed manually before anything is made public.

Who uses it

This tool is used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account. It is not offered to, or used by, the general public.

Google API access

The tool requests a single YouTube permission (youtube.upload), used only to upload videos to the developer's own channel. See the Privacy Policy for details on how data is handled.
